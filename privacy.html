Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026.

Tenessy is a personal application used only by its developer and owner, on the owner's own accounts.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, Tenessy accesses the owner's Gmail messages (read-only scope, or send-only scope for sending) through the Gmail API.

How data is used

Email content is used only to show it to the owner, summarize it for the owner, and to send messages the owner has explicitly approved. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with any third party.

Where data is stored

Access tokens are stored on the owner's private server. Email content is not stored permanently and is not transferred to anyone other than the owner. Summaries may be processed by an AI assistant that the owner operates.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Tenessy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
